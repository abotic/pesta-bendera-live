Pesta Bendera Live

An interactive live minigame that runs on our own YouTube channels. Viewers type their country in the live chat and their country's character joins the next round of a short cartoon minigame; viewers vote for the next game and answer quiz questions.

Live on: PaisesParlanchines, Muat Nggak?, Indonesia vs Dunia.
