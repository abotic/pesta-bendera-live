Pesta Bendera Live - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Pesta Bendera Live is an internal tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channels listed below ("we") to run an interactive live minigame on our own YouTube channels (PaisesParlanchines, Muat Nggak?, Indonesia vs Dunia). It uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our live streams you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access

  • Live chat messages posted in our own broadcasts (message text, author display name, author channel ID, publish time), and moderation events (deleted messages, banned users) - via the YouTube Data API.
  • Our own channels' recent public subscribers (display name) to show an on-screen thank-you.
  • Our own broadcasts' status and statistics.

How we use it

Chat messages are processed in memory only, to run the game: detecting the country a viewer types, counting votes and quiz answers, and showing display names and short, filtered comments on screen after a short moderation delay. Messages deleted by YouTube or a moderator, and banned users, are removed from the screen.

What we store

Only an aggregated daily ranking (wins per country and the display names of winning players) is stored locally on our computer for the on-screen leaderboard. It is deleted after 30 days. We do not store chat message texts.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. We do not use the data for advertising.

Your choices

You can stop interacting at any time by not posting in our live chat. Our tool authorizes only our own channels; if you ever granted access to an application, you can revoke it at Google security settings.

Contact

Please contact us through the contact options on the "About" page of our YouTube channels: PaisesParlanchines, Muat Nggak?, Indonesia vs Dunia.
