Pesta Bendera Live - Terms of Use

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Pesta Bendera Live is an internal tool of the owner of the YouTube channels PaisesParlanchines, Muat Nggak? and Indonesia vs Dunia. It runs an interactive live minigame on these channels and uses YouTube API Services.

Participation

Viewers take part only by writing in the live chat of our broadcasts on YouTube. By doing so you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and YouTube's Community Guidelines; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What is shown

The game may show your public YouTube display name, your chosen country and a short, filtered comment on the live stream. Messages are filtered and delayed for moderation; we may hide any message or name at our discretion. Deleted messages and banned users are removed.

No prizes, no payments

The game is free entertainment. There are no prizes, bets, purchases or rewards for subscribing, liking or playing.
